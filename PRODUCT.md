# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Qatar-based construction, infrastructure, industrial and demolition decision-makers —
project managers, procurement officers, site engineers and contractors — sourcing
heavy equipment rental and contracting services. They arrive with a task ("I need a
50T crane next week", "who can move this plant across Doha") and need to judge
competence, availability and trust fast, often on mobile, sometimes in Arabic.

## Product Purpose

A premium corporate marketing site for a heavy-equipment rental & contracting company
(placeholder brand: RASIKH). It exists to convert enquiries: communicate a modern
fleet, safe operations and single-source capability, then route to a quote. Success =
credible first impression + a completed quote/WhatsApp/phone enquiry.

## Brand Personality

Rugged · premium · dependable. The voice is confident and plain-spoken — a serious
operator, not a flashy startup. Heavy without being dark; industrial without being
cheap. Trust is earned through specificity (real capacities, safety method, clear
terms), never through hype or invented claims.

## Anti-references

- SaaS-cream landing pages with pastel gradients and floating 3D blobs.
- Generic contractor templates: stock "handshake" hero, ghost cards, endless identical
  icon+heading+text grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section.
- Over-rounded, over-shadowed "friendly" UI. This is plant hire, not a wellness app.
- Fabricated trust (fake clients/testimonials/certs/stats). Everything shown is real
  or clearly editable.

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity is the trust signal.** Real capacities, real method, real contact —
   never invented numbers. Concrete beats adjectives.
2. **Weight, not decoration.** Hierarchy from scale/weight/graphite mass, not from
   eyebrows, stripes and shadow tricks.
3. **Show the machines.** A heavy-equipment brand must show real equipment; imagery
   carries the weight, chrome supports it.
4. **Fast and legible on a dusty phone in sunlight.** Performance and contrast are
   brand attributes here, not afterthoughts.
5. **Bilingual by right.** English and Arabic RTL are first-class, not bolted on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Verified focus states, keyboard operability, `role="alert"` /
`aria-live` form feedback, ≥4.5:1 body contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored,
44px+ touch targets, full RTL support for Arabic.
