# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Saudi and GCC industrial buyers sourcing heavy equipment and transportation:
project managers, procurement officers, site engineers, plant and logistics
coordinators working on oil & gas, petrochemical, infrastructure and power
projects. They arrive with a concrete task — *"I need a 400 T crawler crane for a
vessel lift in Yanbu"*, *"who can move a tracked excavator to Jubail next week"* —
and need to judge capability, capacity range and credibility in seconds. Often on
a phone, on site, in bright sun, sometimes in Arabic.

## Product Purpose

The corporate site for **Jowain Yanbu Est.** (est. 1992, Yanbu Al Bahr). It
exists to convert enquiries: state the equipment groups and the real capacity
ranges, show 30+ years of standing and Kingdom-wide coverage, then route the
visitor into a request. Success = a credible first impression plus a completed
equipment request.

## Brand Personality

Established · engineered · dependable. The voice of an operator with three
decades of plant on the ground: plain, specific, unhurried. Heavy without being
gloomy; industrial without being cheap. Trust is earned through specificity —
real tonnage ranges, named equipment groups, qualified operators — never through
hype.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing pages: pastel gradients, floating blobs, glass everywhere.
- Generic contractor templates: stock handshake hero, ghost cards, endless
  identical icon+heading+text grids, a tiny uppercase eyebrow above every
  section.
- Over-rounded, over-shadowed "friendly" UI. This is plant hire, not a wellness
  app.
- Fabricated trust: invented project counts, unverified certifications,
  testimonials nobody gave, phone numbers nobody published.
- AI-deck imagery. The supplied company profile contains AI-generated machinery
  photographs with impossible geometry; none of them ship.

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity is the trust signal.** 20–1200 T, 25–120 T, 55–3200 T. Where a
   figure is unknown the site says "Available on request" rather than filling the
   gap.
2. **Absence over invention.** Every missing fact removes UI instead of
   generating content: no phone number means no call button, not a fake one.
3. **The image must match the machine.** A crawler crane card never shows a
   mobile crane. Equipment with no verified photograph gets an engineered panel.
4. **Light surface, dark anchors.** White and cool blue-grey carry the site;
   deep navy carries the hero, the "why us" section and the conversion bands.
5. **Motion is feedback, not decoration.** A 12px reveal, a 4.5° card tilt, a
   10px hero parallax — all of it degrades to nothing under reduced motion or
   without JavaScript.
6. **Arabic is not a translation layer.** Every content object is bilingual and
   typed; the Arabic route renders Arabic everywhere or the build fails.

## Conversion path

Hero → equipment group → equipment detail (capacity, applications, spec) →
Request Equipment (prefilled with that unit) → recorded enquiry.

Secondary paths: the published email address, and the fleet index for browsing by
category.
